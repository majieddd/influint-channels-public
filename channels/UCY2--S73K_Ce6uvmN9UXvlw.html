The repeatable shapes this channel publishes, written as templates. Ordered by how well the shape performs against the channel’s own median, not by how often it is used: a format shipped twenty times that underperforms is a habit, not a strength. The multiplier is the group’s median outlier score, measured against each video’s contemporaries. A shape marked era-bound ran in a block — most of its neighbouring uploads share the same shape, so its score compares it against itself and cannot be read as performance. Interleaving two formats over the same months is what makes them comparable.
