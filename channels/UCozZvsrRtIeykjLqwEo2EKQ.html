Formats

The repeatable shapes this channel publishes, written as templates. A format is a shape a viewer recognises as “another one of those” — so two videos belong together when they make the same promise, even if they share no words. Ordered by how well the shape performs against the channel’s own median, not by how often it is used: a format shipped twenty times that underperforms is a habit, not a strength. The multiplier is the group’s median outlier score, measured against each video’s contemporaries. A shape marked era-bound ran in a block — most of its neighbouring uploads share the same shape, so its score compares it against itself and cannot be read as performance. Interleaving two formats over the same months is what makes them comparable.

Clock time lets each curve stop where its video stops, so length and endurance become visible. On the % axis the dashed white line is this channel's own median — the bar every video is judged against; clock time has no shared x, so no median is drawn. By upload date adds a third axis: each plane is one slice of the channel’s history, receding by when it was published — oldest at the back. Each plane draws the MEDIAN curve of the uploads in its window, not every video: a hundred planes land a pixel apart and stack into a solid wall, and the question is whether retention drifted, not what one video did. Median rather than mean, so a single breakout cannot speak for a whole quarter. Window narrows the set: the last N uploads holds the sample size constant and answers “has my recent work changed”, while the last N days answers “what has this channel been doing lately” and lets a quiet stretch show up as a near-empty chart rather than as a flattering handful of curves. Length and Views slide over PERCENTILES of the catalogue rather than raw numbers, because views are heavy-tailed and a linear slider would spend nine tenths of its travel between the pack and one breakout; the label always shows the real threshold.
