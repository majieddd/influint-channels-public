Packaging prioritiesWhat to test next
  1. Keep the organization anchor, add a distinct task. Retain “organize with me” familiarity, then name the closet, storage setup or restock problem. A strong category median does not mean every upload needs the same title.
  2. Keep the makeover series, clarify each episode’s payoff. The new Part 2 result challenges a blanket rule against renovation or numbered episodes. Use the actual A-frame, room or project as the visual anchor. Its public description covers demolition, painting and exterior progress, so avoid promising a completed whole-house reveal.
  3. Treat travel as a fit question as well as a packaging question. The travel cohort’s 0.62x median is based on only 8 videos. Make the family experience or useful home-related angle explicit when it exists in the footage. A new thumbnail cannot guarantee that the core homemaking audience wants a destination vlog.
  4. Test the older candidates before claiming a lift. The study pairs 22 existing uploads with proposed titles and After concepts. Its first group has eight candidates. Their scores and retention belong to the original uploads; the proposed packages have no measured performance yet.
