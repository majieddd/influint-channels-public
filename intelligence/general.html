Candidate Law ideation · checked across 46 channels
Demand evidence from competitors requires at least five independent examples sharing the same viewer promise but differing in creator, execution and upload date — one outlier is luck.
Source: idea-engine research dossier
Evidence and caveats →

Tested — supported: Only 11 of 57 stored formats reach 5+ scored evidence videos. 22 of 57 have all evidence from a single channel, so they fail the independence test outright. The formats that fail the bar are precisely the ones carrying the headline numbers: of the 16 formats reporting >=2x lift, 12 rest on <=3 examples and 6 are single-channel. Spearman(distinct channels, lift) = +0.10 — channel diversity buys essentially no lift, meaning a format's high number is not coming from breadth of evidence. And the thin high-lift formats do not survive their own evidence: dropping one instance takes the >=2x group from 2.31x to 1.82x.

Caveat: We can show that thin, single-creator evidence produces unstable lift estimates; we cannot show that a 5-example bar would have produced better-performing ideas, because no ideas were published under both rules. The specific number five is not itself validated — three independent channels would likely capture most of the gain.

Collect at least five examples that share a viewer promise but differ in creator, execution, and upload date.

Theory ideation

Source: idea-engine research dossier
What would settle it

Which layer are you generating at — a NEW FORMAT or a NEW EPISODE inside a proven one? Fixation is a risk only where the output is supposed to be structurally novel; where the output is deliberately a variant of a known structure, exposure is not contamination, it is the input. The operationally useful split: run the anti-fixation freeze at the format/premise layer, and run outlier study at the topic/variable layer — which is precisely Marcus Jones's own asymmetry (topics inside, formats outside) read one level up. Erik Bork (02de88eed921) marks the third position: once you have generated, WATCH the competitor's version rather than avoiding it, because the differences are what make yours viable. So the rule is sequenced, not opposed — the store currently holds three timestamps of one process as three competing doctrines.
