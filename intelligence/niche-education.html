Theory packaging
Titles that name a self-contradictory identity ('If You're Creative But Lazy', 'If You're Ambitious But Lazy Please Watch This') outperform single-trait callouts because the viewer feels specifically recognised in a contradiction they believed was private.
Source: Creator Hooks
