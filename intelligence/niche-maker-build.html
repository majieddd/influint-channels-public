ideation 9

Law ideation · earlier check spanned 8 channels
A repeatable maker format is: take a thing everyone already understands (an office, a kitchen) and install it in an unused domestic crevice — the format inherits the 'secret/hidden' appeal that is currently strong on YouTube, and it addresses small-home viewers who outnumber large-home viewers.
Source: internal strategy session
Reviewed by the team’s strategist

this is a great take

Predates the measurement ledger — a single manual pass, not a repeated test. With nothing measured yet, this check is the only evidence behind the standing above.

Supported: Corpus: WITH n=26 median 1.56x vs 1.00x (MWU z=3.15, p=0.0016); age-adjusted 1.19x vs 1.00x (z=2.24, p=0.025); 5 of 6 channels with >=2 matching videos came out ahead (CashBlox 4.30x vs 0.98x, Motovudu 4.44x vs 0.95x, Itchy Boots 1.27x vs 0.99x, Niz & Rex 1.50x vs 0.92x, Lana's Life 1.05x vs 0.88x). Caveat: The corpus effect shrinks sharply once age is controlled (1.56x to 1.19x), so the headline number overstates it. retention 14

Theory retention
Deliberately withholding the finished result from the intro — showing a blueprint overlay instead of the built object — keeps viewers watching longer than showing the payoff up front, because it preserves the open question.
Source: CreatorNow
Reconciled with a conflicting claim

Another claim in this base says something incompatible, and it is not a contradiction — the two apply to different kinds of channel. The question that separates them:

Does the video's promise turn on WHETHER IT WORKS (feasibility — withholding preserves the question) or on WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE (spectacle — proof of delivery is the click-confirmation), and does the thumbnail already show the finished object? If the thumbnail shows it, withholding is impossible and reads as a bait-and-switch; if the thumbnail poses a question, showing the object closes it. Restated: (a) thumbnail shows the finished object → first frame must partially deliver it; (b) thumbnail poses a feasibility question → withhold the object, show the plan.
