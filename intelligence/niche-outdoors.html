Source: internal strategy session

Another claim in this base says something incompatible, and it is not a contradiction — the two apply to different kinds of channel. The question that separates them:

Is the audience's attachment to the PERSON or to the CONTENT CONTRACT — and does the variety span different subjects inside one relationship, or different viewer JOBS (entertain me vs teach me vs let me follow you) on one feed?
