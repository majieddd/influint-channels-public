01 · The channel in numbers

A weekly channel with one proven shape and a wide catalogue around it

Two hundred uploads from August 2022 to August 2026, one every Tuesday (172 of 200), median runtime 22 minutes, median lifetime views about 101k, 335k subscribers. No analytics grant, so everything here is public data: lifetime views, titles, thumbnails, runtimes, and the spoken openings from public captions. Scores are lifetime views against the uploads nearest in time (1.0x = typical for the channel then), which is fair on a weekly channel because neighbours share their accrual time.

The catalogue splits cleanly. One shape, the organization collage, holds 42 uploads at a median 1.88x and every one of the eight biggest uploads of the four years. Everything else is a different promise wearing the same template: cleaning routines at 1.00x, decorating and makeovers at 0.87 to 0.93x, house tours and trips at 0.60x.

What works, in one sentence. Long, dense, multi-space organizing with a restock or a declutter in it, packaged as a nine-tile collage with one giant line of white type, uploaded every Tuesday.
What does not. Anything where the subject is the family, a place or a project rather than the organizing: trips, tours, life updates, serial “Part N” markers, and short routine videos.

03 · Titles

The template never changes, so the lead is the title

Her titles follow one template: LEAD!!😍 VERB WITH ME | DECLUTTERING AND ORGANIZING MOTIVATION YEAR. Thirteen words, 93% with an emoji, and the tail is identical across shapes. The only thing that varies is the lead, and the lead predicts the score: “Home organization ideas” at 1.96x over 39 uploads, “Whole house clean with me” at 1.22x, decorating and makeovers below 1.0x, trips at half. Two words matter on their own: “declutter” (1.71x with, 0.90x without) and “restock” (1.79x, four uploads).

Runtime is the other lever, and it is stronger than any word: rank correlation +0.54, with the 30-to-45-minute band at 2.44x and the two longest uploads the biggest of their years. The fleet's measured claim says longer videos do not reduce views; this channel says they multiply them.

04 · Thumbnails

A texture, not a subject, and it works

Every top thumbnail is the same object: eight to twelve tiles of organized spaces (matching jars, labelled containers, baskets on white shelving, a stocked fridge), herself in three to five of them working in a dress or apron, one giant line of white condensed type with a black stroke across the middle, a lighter sub-line (“& RESTOCK”, “MARATHON”, “& DECLUTTER”), a year badge and the 😍. The 9.26x, 8.96x and 6.74x uploads are indistinguishable at a glance from the 1.5x ones, which is the point: the collage is a texture that says “a lot of organizing happens here”, and the density is the promise.

That runs against the store's warning about crowded thumbnails (789bf7d1054c), and the honest reading is that the warning is about subjects that must be recognised at scroll size; her thumbnail is recognised as a category, not a subject, by an audience that browses for exactly this. The weak thumbnails confirm it from the other side: the Alaska trip (0.46x) and the Oregon house tour (0.66x) are the same collage built from landscapes and people, and “10 EASY STEPS” (0.57x) swapped the texture for a listicle.

06 · Why

The mechanisms, tied to what the store has measured

  • The variable is the space, not the words. Warning cd5c43fc4889 names “format without variables” as a failure: branding that repeats while episodes contain nothing new. Her organization shape avoids it by changing the room, the season and the restock; the weekly clean shape (31 videos at 1.00x) is the same template with no variable, and the audience treats it as such.
  • Runtime multiplies. The fleet's measured claims (be05cf1f696d, 2be0760e3daa) establish that length does not cost views; this channel's +0.54 rank correlation and its 2.44x thirty-minute band go further. A viewer who chose a “clean and organize with me” video is choosing company for a chore, and more company is more value.
  • Packaging over retention. Law 63223d2e9878: packaging outweighs retention as a driver of views. There are no curves here to argue with, and the score gaps between identical-looking videos with different leads say the click is where the difference is made. Law 7563e845b615 says clickability is usually the binding constraint; nothing in this record contradicts it.
  • Repetition proves the shape. Law 0cf8b8fc71fe: one winner is not a format. Forty-two uploads at a median 1.88x is a format; the four restock uploads at 1.79x are a promising lead that needs ten.
  • The audience is one promise wide. Trips, tours and life updates are packaged for a different viewer (warnings 1a168484b6e0, b593a6831f7e); the collage cannot rescue them because the collage is the promise they break.
  • Rhythm. The cadence claim (6bf44bfcf572, candidate-law, held on 14 of 34 readings) is one she has never tested by breaking: every Tuesday for four years.
