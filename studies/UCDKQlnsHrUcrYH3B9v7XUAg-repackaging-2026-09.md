# Camping with Sam Bananas: repackaging review

Updated 24 September 2026. Catalogue 4 September 2026; 188 produced uploads.

The current web study keeps the original data in expandable tiles and compares all 23 packages with identical observed browse metadata. After images are AI-generated concepts, not actual footage or tested outcomes.

## Evidence and limits

- Rain format: 1.96x median (n=43). Rain keyword: 1.64x (n=63). Any weather: 1.45x (n=98), versus no weather 0.73x (n=90).
- 41,436 median lifetime views; 37 uploads at 2x or more; 36 below 0.5x.
- No Studio CTR, impressions, first-28-day windows or retention curves. Likes per view is not retention.
- Similar-location uploads are observational comparisons, not natural experiments proving packaging effects.
- The budget tutorial, old-dog cabin trip and Hawaii road trip have weak or invalid format proxies; review fit separately. Rain Forests does not establish rain weather.
