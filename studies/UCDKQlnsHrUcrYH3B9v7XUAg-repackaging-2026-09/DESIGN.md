# Influint repackaging study design

This revision applies the requested before/after review to the existing Influint visual identity. It is a targeted redesign.

DESIGN_VARIANCE: 3/10
MOTION_INTENSITY: 1/10
VISUAL_DENSITY: 5/10

## Tokens and typography

study.css is the token source: ground #090f10, panel #102020, raised #182c2c, text #eff5f4, muted #a9bab8, accent #8dd8d3, line #314b4b, control #769693. Amber #ead194 means Review format fit only. These are tinted extensions of the existing ink/teal contract.

Poppins carries the Influint page, headlines and analysis. Roboto carries the YouTube-style title, channel name, view metadata and duration. This supersedes the former Poppins-only rule because the user requested YouTube formatting. The existing Influint mark was restored from repository snapshot 1996a74c363d637b43a12f2e673ade4eea33d897. The channel avatar comes from the source channel page, downloaded 24 September 2026.

Type scale: 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 26, 32 to 52px. Spacing: 8, 16, 24, 40px; 12px is the compact control gap. Radius: 6px controls, 12px thumbnails and data tiles. Avatar diameter 36px desktop and 32px mobile.

## Components

- Eight native details tiles at the top; four columns desktop and two on phones. Expanded data spans the full grid width.
- Two equal YouTube-style video packages per comparison. On phones, Before is immediately followed by After for the same upload.
- Both packages share actual recorded views, age and duration. No forecast counts or fake verification badges.
- One hairline between candidates, no decorative texture.
- Search and priority filters enhance complete static HTML. All cards and evidence remain readable without JavaScript.
- AI images are labeled concepts with provenance and content-check limits.
- Links and buttons have focus rings. No animation is needed; reduced-motion fallback is explicit.

## Boundaries

Do not invent storms, prices, temperatures, encounters or outcomes in titles. Preserve all 23 entries, including weak format matches marked for review. Do not turn likes/view into retention or observational comparisons into causal proof.
