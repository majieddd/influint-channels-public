# Nizar IRL study visual contract

Targeted revision of the existing black, teal and Poppins Influint study. Keep the brand and reference imagery. Seven chapters follow the production workflow; Ideation is first and contains all 100 stable idea IDs.

Navigation stays visible and scrolls horizontally on mobile. Ten ideas per page limit initial reading depth; search, family and evidence filters cover the complete set. Direct idea links reveal the relevant page. The All 100 option expands the catalogue.

Each card shows one summary sentence and three opening beats. Evidence and source-thumbnail comparisons expand on demand. Costs link to shared profiles. Public performance, Studio retention, historical projections and creative hypotheses use explicit labels.

Teal: links and selected navigation. Green: qualifying outlier / source-tagged peak. Amber: evidence gap or inspection cursor. Coral: source-tagged drop. All statuses have text labels.

No decorative animation. Native dialog supports Escape, focus return, arrow navigation and downloads. Verify 375, 768 and 1280 pixel widths, text contrast, focus, filters, direct links and chart controls in the browser.
